About Us

The Journey Behind Magical Ascents

Founded in 2010, we are a team of passionate Sherpas and adventure specialists dedicated to crafting authentic Himalayan experiences that transform lives. What started as a small group sharing beloved trails has grown into one of Nepal's most trusted trekking companies.

We don't just guide you through the mountains — we immerse you in the culture, introduce you to remote villages, and walk alongside you on a journey that stays with you long after you return home. Every detail is handled so you can focus on the adventure.

Sherpa Led Guides

Responsible Tourism
